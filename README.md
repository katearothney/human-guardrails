# Human Guardrails

*How does AI affect the human capacities we care about, and what guardrails keep them intact?*

An open, evolving exploration of the ways AI can quietly reshape how we think, remember, decide, create, and connect, and the safeguards (personal, product, and policy) that could protect what matters.

This is thinking in public. It is grounded in research where research exists, honest where it does not, and meant to be argued with. I am not claiming to have this figured out.

> **A note on honesty.** The science here is real but young and mixed. Some findings are solid; many are correlational, small-sample, or preprints; several older "tech rots your brain" claims have failed to replicate. Used well, these same tools can *augment* the capacities below rather than erode them. I grade the evidence for every claim and include counter-evidence on purpose. Treat this as a map of an open question, not a verdict. All views here are my own.

## The question
We adopt tools faster than we understand what they do to us. AI is unusually intimate: it does not just store our information, it does some of our thinking, remembering, deciding, and even our talking-to-people. Some of that is a gift. Some of it may quietly weaken capacities that took a lifetime, or a species, to build. This repo tries to be specific: which capacities are at stake, what the evidence actually shows, and what we can do, as individuals, builders, and institutions, to keep the human parts human.

## The framework
Every capacity gets looked at four ways:

1. **The capacity** — what it is, and why it is worth protecting.
2. **How AI can disrupt it** — the mechanism.
3. **What the evidence says** — graded honestly (strong / moderate / preliminary), with counter-evidence.
4. **Human guardrails** — at three levels: personal practices, product and design patterns, and policy.

## The capacities

| Capacity | How AI can disrupt it | One guardrail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Effortful / critical thinking](capacities/effortful-thinking.md)** | Offloading the hard part before you have struggled with it | Generate *after* you think, not before | Emerging |
| Memory & internalized knowledge | Remembering where to look instead of what you know | Retrieve from your own memory first | Mixed |
| Attention & focus | Fragmentation and the pull of the feed | Single-task by default | Contested |
| Independent judgment | Automation bias, anchoring on the model's answer | Decide first, then consult | Moderate |
| Creativity & originality | Individually boosted, collectively homogenized | Draft before you prompt | Moderate |
| Social connection & empathy | Frictionless AI company standing in for effortful human ties | Keep AI additive to people, not a substitute | Mixed |
| Epistemic autonomy | Sycophancy telling you what you want to hear | Ask the model to disagree with you | Moderate |

**Start here:** [Effortful / critical thinking](capacities/effortful-thinking.md) is the fully worked example. The rest are sketched above and will be built out over time, same structure, same evidence grading.
