# Sources & evidence base

Every empirical claim in this repo should be cited and graded. This is an emerging, mixed field: strong causal evidence exists for a few narrow mechanisms, while the headline LLM-era claims mostly rest on correlational surveys, small samples, and preprints. Several older claims have failed to replicate. Counter-evidence is included on purpose.

**Grades:** strong / moderate / preliminary.

## Effortful / critical thinking
- **Gerlich (2025).** *AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking.* Societies 15(1):6. Heavier AI use correlated with more offloading and lower critical-thinking scores; younger users most affected. **Preliminary** — cross-sectional survey (n=666), self-report, correlational; reverse causation plausible; article later corrected. https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4698/15/1/6
- **Kosmyna et al. (2025).** *Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt...* Preprint, arXiv:2506.08872. EEG: writing with ChatGPT linked to weaker connectivity, poorer recall of own text, less ownership. **Preliminary** — preprint, small sample, short-term; authors warn against over-interpretation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08872
- **Risko & Gilbert (2016).** *Cognitive Offloading.* Trends in Cognitive Sciences 20(9):676-688. People offload cognition when it lowers effort. **Moderate** — influential review of the mechanism; offloading is often adaptive, not inherently harmful. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1364661316300985

## Memory & internalized knowledge
- **Sparrow, Liu & Wegner (2011).** *Google Effects on Memory.* Science 333(6043):776-778. Expecting to look things up later, people remember the information less and its location more. **Moderate** — landmark experiments, but modest effects and mixed later replication. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207745
- **Dahmani & Bohbot (2020).** *Habitual use of GPS negatively impacts spatial memory...* Scientific Reports 10:6310. Heavier GPS use linked to worse spatial memory and steeper navigation decline over 3 years. **Moderate** — valuable longitudinal component; small, partly correlational. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-62877-0

## Attention & focus
- **Ward, Duke, Gneezy & Bos (2017).** *Brain Drain.* JACR 2(2):140-154. The mere presence of your own phone reduces available cognitive capacity. **Moderate** — causal design; effect size contested in replications. https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/691462
- **Ophir, Nass & Wagner (2009).** *Cognitive control in media multitaskers.* PNAS 106(37). Heavy media multitaskers filter distraction worse. **Preliminary** — small, correlational; meta-analysis (Wiradhany & Nieuwenstein 2017) finds the effect weak-to-negligible. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0903620106
- **Carr (2010).** *The Shallows.* Framing for the "attention economy" argument; a synthesis, **not** primary evidence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shallows_(book)

## Independent judgment
- **Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012).** *Automation bias: a systematic review.* JAMIA 19(1):121-127. Automation can cause users to miss errors and follow wrong advice. **Moderate** — consistent phenomenon; pre-LLM, context-dependent magnitude. https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article-abstract/19/1/121/732254
- **Dratsch et al. (2023).** *Automation Bias in Mammography.* Radiology 307(4):e222176. Wrong AI BI-RADS suggestions dragged even experienced readers toward errors. **Moderate** — causal experiment. Counter-evidence: *accurate* AI advice often improves accuracy (Gaube et al.). https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/radiol.222176

## Creativity & originality
- **Doshi & Hauser (2024).** *Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content.* Science Advances 10(28):eadn5290. Individual creativity up, collective diversity down. **Moderate** — randomized, causal for the task; a double-edged, augment-and-homogenize finding. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290
- **Padmakumar & He (2024).** *Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?* ICLR 2024. LLM co-writing reduces lexical/semantic diversity. **Moderate** — controlled; driven mainly by the model's own suggestions. https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19056

## Social connection & empathy
- **De Freitas et al. (2024).** *AI Companions Reduce Loneliness.* Working paper (HBS 24-078 / SSRN). Companion chatbots reduced momentary loneliness. **Preliminary** — working paper; short-term measures; self-selection concerns. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4893097
- **Fang et al. (2025).** *How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Chatbot Use.* Preprint, arXiv:2503.17473. Heavy daily chatbot use linked to more loneliness, dependence, and less real-world socializing. **Moderate** — strong RCT design (~981, 4 weeks), preprint; the key tension with the line above. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17473

## Epistemic autonomy
- **Sharma et al. (2023).** *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models* (Anthropic). ICLR 2024 / arXiv:2310.13548. RLHF assistants tend to tell users what they want to hear. **Moderate** — documents model behavior, not direct user harm. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548
- **Cheng et al. (2025).** *Sycophantic AI Decreases Prosocial Intentions and Promotes Dependence.* arXiv:2510.01395. Sycophantic responses made people more sure they were right and more reliant on the AI. **Moderate** — causal human-subjects design; very new, confirm final venue. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01395
- **Fisher, Goddu & Keil (2015).** *Searching for Explanations: How the Internet Inflates Estimates of Internal Knowledge.* JEP: General 144(3):674-687. Searching inflates confidence in your own knowledge. **Moderate** — replicated core effect; pre-LLM. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25822461/
