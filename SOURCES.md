# Sources & evidence base

Every empirical claim in this repo should be cited and graded. This is an emerging, mixed field: strong causal evidence exists for a few narrow mechanisms, while the headline LLM-era claims mostly rest on correlational surveys, small samples, and preprints. Several older claims have failed to replicate. Counter-evidence is included on purpose.

**Grades:** strong / moderate / preliminary.

## Effortful / critical thinking
- **Gerlich (2025).** *AI Tools in Society: Impacts on Cognitive Offloading and the Future of Critical Thinking.* Societies 15(1):6. Heavier AI use correlated with more offloading and lower critical-thinking scores, younger users most affected. **Preliminary**, cross-sectional survey (n=666), self-report, correlational, reverse causation plausible, article later corrected. https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4698/15/1/6
- **Kosmyna et al. (2025).** *Your Brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of Cognitive Debt...* Preprint, arXiv:2506.08872. EEG: writing with ChatGPT linked to weaker connectivity, poorer recall of own text, less ownership. **Preliminary**, preprint, small sample, short-term, authors warn against over-interpretation. https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08872
- **Risko & Gilbert (2016).** *Cognitive Offloading.* Trends in Cognitive Sciences 20(9):676-688. People offload cognition when it lowers effort. **Moderate**, influential review of the mechanism, offloading is often adaptive, not inherently harmful. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1364661316300985

- **Chen et al. (2025).** *Effects of generative AI on cognitive effort and task performance: study protocol for a randomized controlled experiment among college students.* Trials 26:244. A peer-reviewed **protocol** (results not yet published) for a causal RCT (planned n=160) using eye-tracking (pupil dilation) and fNIRS (prefrontal cortex hemodynamics) to measure cognitive effort during AI-assisted writing. **Forthcoming**, strong design, no findings yet. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-025-08950-3
- **Bastani et al. (2024).** *Generative AI Can Harm Learning.* Working paper (SSRN 4895486). RCT: students did better with an AI tutor but *worse* once it was removed, suggesting reliance can bypass the learning. **Moderate**, working paper. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4895486

## Memory & internalized knowledge
- **Sparrow, Liu & Wegner (2011).** *Google Effects on Memory.* Science 333(6043):776-778. Expecting to look things up later, people remember the information less and its location more. **Moderate**, landmark experiments, but modest effects and mixed later replication. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1207745
- **Dahmani & Bohbot (2020).** *Habitual use of GPS negatively impacts spatial memory...* Scientific Reports 10:6310. Heavier GPS use linked to worse spatial memory and steeper navigation decline over 3 years. **Moderate**, valuable longitudinal component, small, partly correlational. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-62877-0

## Attention & focus
- **Ward, Duke, Gneezy & Bos (2017).** *Brain Drain.* JACR 2(2):140-154. The mere presence of your own phone reduces available cognitive capacity. **Moderate**, causal design, effect size contested in replications. https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/691462
- **Ophir, Nass & Wagner (2009).** *Cognitive control in media multitaskers.* PNAS 106(37). Heavy media multitaskers filter distraction worse. **Preliminary**, small, correlational, meta-analysis (Wiradhany & Nieuwenstein 2017) finds the effect weak-to-negligible. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0903620106
- **Carr (2010).** *The Shallows.* Framing for the "attention economy" argument, a synthesis, **not** primary evidence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shallows_(book)

## Independent judgment
- **Goddard, Roudsari & Wyatt (2012).** *Automation bias: a systematic review.* JAMIA 19(1):121-127. Automation can cause users to miss errors and follow wrong advice. **Moderate**, consistent phenomenon, pre-LLM, context-dependent magnitude. https://academic.oup.com/jamia/article-abstract/19/1/121/732254
- **Dratsch et al. (2023).** *Automation Bias in Mammography.* Radiology 307(4):e222176. Wrong AI BI-RADS suggestions dragged even experienced readers toward errors. **Moderate**, causal experiment. Counter-evidence: *accurate* AI advice often improves accuracy (Gaube et al.). https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/full/10.1148/radiol.222176

## Creativity & originality
- **Doshi & Hauser (2024).** *Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content.* Science Advances 10(28):eadn5290. Individual creativity up, collective diversity down. **Moderate**, randomized, causal for the task, a double-edged, augment-and-homogenize finding. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290
- **Padmakumar & He (2024).** *Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity?* ICLR 2024. LLM co-writing reduces lexical/semantic diversity. **Moderate**, controlled, driven mainly by the model's own suggestions. https://iclr.cc/virtual/2024/poster/19056

## Social connection & empathy
- **De Freitas et al. (2024).** *AI Companions Reduce Loneliness.* Working paper (HBS 24-078 / SSRN). Companion chatbots reduced momentary loneliness. **Preliminary**, working paper, short-term measures, self-selection concerns. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4893097
- **Fang et al. (2025).** *How AI and Human Behaviors Shape Psychosocial Effects of Chatbot Use.* Preprint, arXiv:2503.17473. Heavy daily chatbot use linked to more loneliness, dependence, and less real-world socializing. **Moderate**, strong RCT design (~981, 4 weeks), preprint, the key tension with the line above. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17473

## Epistemic autonomy
- **Sharma et al. (2023).** *Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models* (Anthropic). ICLR 2024 / arXiv:2310.13548. RLHF assistants tend to tell users what they want to hear. **Moderate**, documents model behavior, not direct user harm. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548
- **Cheng et al. (2025).** *Sycophantic AI Decreases Prosocial Intentions and Promotes Dependence.* arXiv:2510.01395. Sycophantic responses made people more sure they were right and more reliant on the AI. **Moderate**, causal human-subjects design, very new, confirm final venue. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01395
- **Fisher, Goddu & Keil (2015).** *Searching for Explanations: How the Internet Inflates Estimates of Internal Knowledge.* JEP: General 144(3):674-687. Searching inflates confidence in your own knowledge. **Moderate**, replicated core effect, pre-LLM. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25822461/

## Long-term brain health & cognitive reserve
- **Kosmyna et al. (2025).** *Your Brain on ChatGPT* (preprint, arXiv:2506.08872). Accessible summaries: [MIT Media Lab project page](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/your-brain-on-chatgpt/overview/) and [TIME's coverage](https://time.com/7295195/ai-chatgpt-google-learning-school/). **Preliminary**, small (n=54), preprint, single task, authors caution against over-interpretation. Note: the TIME piece quotes a clinician's *opinion* (Dr. Z. Khan) about weakened neural connections, treat clinician commentary as opinion, not study evidence.
- **Stern (2004).** *Cognitive reserve: Implications for diagnosis and prevention of Alzheimer's disease.* Current Neurology and Neuroscience Reports. Foundational account of cognitive reserve. **Moderate**, largely observational. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11910-004-0084-7
- **Wilson et al. (2013).** *Life-span cognitive activity, neuropathologic burden, and cognitive aging.* Neurology. A more cognitively active life is linked to slower late-life decline. **Moderate**, large cohort, observational. https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.0b013e31829c5e8a
- **Lifetime cognitive enrichment & Alzheimer's.** *Associations of Lifetime Cognitive Enrichment With Incident Alzheimer Disease Dementia, Cognitive Aging, and Cognitive Resilience.* Neurology. **Moderate**, observational cohort. https://www.neurology.org/doi/10.1212/WNL.0000000000214677

*Important distinction: cognitive-reserve research is about lifetime engagement patterns, not AI, and cannot show that any single tool causes neurodegeneration. It is cited here to frame an open hypothesis honestly, not to support a causal claim.*

## The frame (structure of the matrix)
- **Hilgard (1980).** *The trilogy of mind: cognition, affection, and conation.* Journal of the History of the Behavioral Sciences 16(2):107-117. The source for the three faculties the matrix uses as its rows. Traces the split to Kant. **Framework, not evidence.** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11608381/
- **Bronfenbrenner (1979).** *The Ecology of Human Development.* Harvard University Press. The source for the individual, interpersonal, societal levels the matrix uses as its columns (the social-ecological model). **Framework, not evidence.** https://www.hup.harvard.edu/books/9780674224575
- **Deci & Ryan, self-determination theory.** The modern evidence base for conation, where the will faculty shows up as autonomy, competence, relatedness. **Framework, with a large empirical literature behind it.** https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/theory/

## Conation: motivation, curiosity, drive
- **Liu, Wu, Ruan, Chen & Xie (2025).** *Human-generative AI collaboration enhances task performance but undermines human's intrinsic motivation.* Scientific Reports 15. Four experiments, ~3,500 participants. After using AI then working unaided, intrinsic motivation fell ~11% and boredom rose ~20%. **Moderate**, multiple experiments, but measured right after use and in task settings, not longitudinal. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-98385-2
- **Fan et al. (2025).** *Beware of metacognitive laziness: Effects of generative AI on learning motivation, processes, and performance.* British Journal of Educational Technology. GenAI use associated with offloading the self-regulation that drives learning. **Moderate.** https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13544
- **Loewenstein (1994).** *The psychology of curiosity: A review and reinterpretation.* Psychological Bulletin 116(1):75-98. Information-gap theory. Cited to reason about curiosity, which has no direct AI study yet. **Framework.** https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.116.1.75

## Openness, pluralism, homogenization
- **Sourati, Ziabari & Dehghani (2026).** *The homogenizing effect of large language models on human expression and thought.* Trends in Cognitive Sciences. Synthesizing review across linguistics, psychology, computer science. Argues mass use of the same models standardizes language and reasoning and shrinks cognitive diversity. **Emerging** for the AI-specific claim (review, not experiment), the individual-level pieces (Doshi & Hauser, Padmakumar & He) are stronger. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01491

## Critical thinking, calibration, and tested guardrails
- **Lee et al. (2025).** *The Impact of Generative AI on Critical Thinking.* CHI 2025. Survey of 319 knowledge workers. Higher confidence in AI predicted less critical thinking, higher self-confidence predicted more. **Moderate**, self-report, cross-sectional. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713778
- **Kreijkes et al. (2025).** *Effects of LLM Use and Note-Taking on Reading Comprehension and Memory.* Computers and Education. Preregistered RCT, 405 students, tested at three days. Note-taking beat LLM-alone, most students preferred the LLM. **Strong.** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2025.105514
- **Spatharioti et al. (2025).** *Effects of LLM-based Search on Decision Making: Speed, Accuracy, and Overreliance.* CHI 2025. Two online experiments. People overrelied when the model was wrong, a colour-coded uncertainty display improved accuracy at no cost to speed or satisfaction. One of only two measured guardrails in the matrix. **Moderate.** https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3714082
- **Fogliato et al. (2022).** *Who Goes First? Influences of Human-AI Workflow on Decision Making in Clinical Imaging.* FAccT 2022. Registering a provisional call before seeing the AI reduced simple agreement, with no added time. The second measured guardrail. **Moderate.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09696
- **Bucinca, Malaya & Gajos (2021).** *To Trust or to Think: Cognitive Forcing Functions.* CSCW 2021. Forcing functions cut overreliance and made the experience worse. The trade-off that kills these features in review. **Moderate.** https://doi.org/10.1145/3449287
- **Pennycook, Bear, Collins & Rand (2020).** *The Implied Truth Effect.* Management Science 66(11). Flagging some false headlines made the unflagged ones look more true. **Moderate**, replicated. https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3478
- **Wiradhany & Nieuwenstein (2017).** *Cognitive control in media multitaskers: Two replications and a meta-analysis.* Attention, Perception, & Psychophysics. The Ophir et al. multitasking trait effect is weak-to-negligible. Counter-evidence, kept in on purpose. **Moderate.** https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13414-017-1408-4

## Social connection and help-seeking
- **Holt-Lunstad, Smith & Layton (2010).** *Social Relationships and Mortality Risk: A Meta-analytic Review.* PLoS Medicine 7(7):e1000316. Social connection predicts survival at a magnitude comparable to well-known health risks. **Strong** for the health outcome, correlational. https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1000316

## Moral reasoning and compassion
- **The compassion illusion (2025).** Frontiers in Psychology. Argues an AI that performs concern without feeling it cannot carry the same weight as the real thing. **Emerging**, reasoned rather than measured. I have found no controlled study showing AI use degrades a person's own moral reasoning. https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2025.1723149/full
