# Open questions

This is the map of what I am actually wondering about, most of which nobody has answered yet. The matrix grades what the research shows. This file is the opposite: the questions that pull me through the work, tagged to a faculty and graded by how much evidence exists. The status column is the honest heart of it. Most of these are "haven't seen research," and I think naming that is more useful than pretending otherwise.

Status runs three ways. **Early signal** means there is at least one study pointing at it. **Research happening** means the adjacent work exists but not the direct test. **Haven't seen research** means I could not find anyone who has studied it, and I would love to be corrected.

## The questions

| The question I'm sitting with | Faculty | Why it might matter | How you'd test it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| When the tool does the hard part, does the drive to do things yourself fade? | Conation | ownership of the work drops when the effort isn't yours | motivation and boredom before and after unaided work | **Early signal.** Liu et al. found an 11 percent motivation drop and 20 percent more boredom ([Scientific Reports, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-98385-2)) |
| Does curiosity survive instant answers? | Conation | curiosity runs on not knowing, and the gap closes instantly | track whether people ask follow-up questions or stop | **Haven't seen research** |
| Does an agreeable assistant weaken how well you hold a view you disagree with? | Conation | flattery plus personalization narrows what reaches you | argue-the-other-side test after time with a sycophantic versus neutral model | **Research happening** on sycophancy ([Sharma et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548), and [Cheng et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01395)) |
| Does outsourcing hard calls make us worse at making them, morally? | Conation | the same offloading logic as thinking, applied to judgment | whether heavy delegation tracks with weaker moral reasoning | **Haven't seen research** |
| Does asking a machine mean we ask each other less? | Conation | the machine is easier to ask than the person | measure human consultation as AI use rises | **Early signal**, only as side findings ([Fogliato et al., 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09696)) |
| Do we all start to sound and think alike when everyone uses the same few models? | Conation, societal | convergence on one default shrinks cognitive diversity | compare expression and reasoning range across heavy versus light users | **Research happening.** [Sourati et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01491), and [Doshi and Hauser, 2024](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.adn5290) |
| Does leaning on AI to think change the brain itself, not just the skill? | Cognition | cognitive-reserve work links lifelong engagement to slower decline | long-run imaging of heavy versus light users, heavily confounded | **Haven't seen research.** Adjacent only, and not the same thing as neurodegeneration |
| Does feeling fluent with AI make you think you're more capable than you are? | Affect | ease of processing gets misread as understanding | compare felt competence to measured competence | **Early signal.** [Lee et al., 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3706598.3713778) on self-confidence |
| When attachment to an AI forms in weeks, does it fade when you stop? | Affect | onset is measured and fast, cessation is unstudied | the study where people stop, which nobody has run | **Haven't seen research** |
| Does AI companionship change what we expect from real people, including how the genders relate? | Affect | a partner that never pushes back resets the baseline | relationship expectations among heavy versus light companion users over time | **Research happening** through loneliness work ([Fang et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17473)) |
| Can you still explain and stand behind work AI mostly did? | Cognition, conation | accountability needs you to understand your own output | whether people can defend AI-heavy work versus their own | **Haven't seen research** |
| Does writing with AI erode your own voice over months? | Cognition | the model's suggestions narrow your range | how distinct someone's writing stays with versus without AI | **Research happening** on homogenization ([Sourati et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01491)) |
| Does moving tasks to AI keep us more seated and indoors? | Physical, adjacent | behaviour shifts as effort leaves the task | activity and time outdoors as tasks move to AI | **Research happening**, but adjacent, on screen time |
| Do warnings and friction help people rely well, or just rely less? | Cognition | reducing agreement isn't the same as improving judgment | separate accepting-right from rejecting-wrong | **Research happening**, unresolved. The one I most want answered |
| Does a reassuring provenance label stop people from verifying? | Cognition | the implied truth effect, a calming signal replaces the check | does a visible label cut the number who go and verify | **Reasoned, not measured.** I want a direct test |

## How these connect to the matrix

Every question here is anchored to a capacity in [matrix.md](matrix.md). The ones marked "early signal" have earned a grade in the matrix already. The ones marked "haven't seen research" are the empty cells, especially across the conation, interpersonal, and societal parts of the grid. That is not an accident of my reading. It is where the field has not looked yet.

If you know of a study that answers one of these, or you think one of the questions is wrong, open an issue. Correcting this list is the most useful thing anyone could do to it.
